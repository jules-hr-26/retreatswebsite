Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Climate & Nature Leaders Community

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the CNLC platform, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it. If you have questions, email julia@globaloptimism.com.

1. Who we are

The Climate & Nature Leaders Community (CNLC) platform is operated by Global Optimism, a company registered in England and Wales.

For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, Global Optimism is the data controller — the organisation responsible for deciding how your personal data is used.

Contact: julia@globaloptimism.com

We do not collect financial information, passwords, or any special category data (such as health, political views, or ethnicity).

3. Why we collect it and our legal basis

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To operate the platform — verifying you are an CNLC alumni, enabling sign-in, and displaying your profile to other members. Legal basis: legitimate interests (running a private alumni community).
  • To display your profile in the member directory — only if you opt in by ticking "Share with community" in your profile settings. Legal basis: consent.
  • To send event and forum notifications — only if you have joined an event or forum. Legal basis: legitimate interests. You can opt out at any time.
  • To record your consent — we store the date you first agreed to these terms. Legal basis: legal obligation.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Supabase's EU West (Ireland) data centre. Ireland is a member of the European Union, and data transfers between the UK and EU are covered by the UK government's adequacy decision, meaning your data receives the same level of protection as under UK law.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your personal data for as long as you have an active account on the platform. If you request deletion of your account, we will erase all of your personal data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain certain records.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — you can request a copy of all the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure — you can ask us to delete all your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to rectification — you can correct inaccurate data by updating your profile, or by contacting us.
  • Right to portability — you can request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — you can object to us processing your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent (e.g. directory listing), you can withdraw it at any time in your profile settings.

To exercise any of these rights, email julia@globaloptimism.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

The CNLC platform uses a single, strictly necessary cookie — cnlc_session — to keep you signed in. This cookie is HttpOnly and Secure. It does not track your browsing behaviour and is not used for advertising. No third-party tracking scripts or analytics are loaded on this platform.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. All connections are encrypted via HTTPS. Your session token is stored in an HttpOnly cookie (not accessible to JavaScript). Database access is restricted to authorised server-side code only; no personal data is ever exposed directly to the browser.

10. Children

The CNLC platform is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify members by email before the changes take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the current version.

12. How to complain
