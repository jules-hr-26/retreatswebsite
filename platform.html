From our community · Global Gathering 2023

I exist, I am alive, I am here.

I came up with this simple walking gatha after attending the Global Gathering at Plum Village in 2023, my first experience with the community:

One step: I exist.
One step: I am alive.
One step: I am here.

Since then, I have dedicated one specific city block between my home and the gym to this practice. No matter how rushed I am, or even if I am on a phone call, when I reach that block I try to walk it mindfully, repeating these words with each step.

Over time, this practice has become something I can carry with me beyond that block. Whenever I notice myself caught in stress or worry, I return to these simple words. They help me soothe myself.

The practice helps me remember that life is happening now, not after the next meeting or accomplishment. It is a simple reminder to arrive in myself, to come home to myself.

Collective Practice

Formats and tools for bringing the community together — whether for an afternoon or a full day of practice.

1
Gathering
Creating Moments of Practice
For people who want to bring mindfulness into gatherings
Format · 30–60 min
Tea Meditation
Slow down around a shared pot. No agenda — just presence, warmth, and silence between sips.
Format · 45–90 min
Walking Meditation
Move together in silence or with soft spoken guidance — a park, a garden, or a city block.
Format · 2–4 hrs
Mindful Hike
Slower than a regular hike — pausing to notice, breathe, and arrive at each step.
Format · 2–4 hrs
Forest Bathing
A sensory slow walk guided by the Japanese practice of shinrin-yoku.
Format · 45–60 min
Silent Meal
Eat together in silence with the five contemplations, then close with a brief sharing.
Format · Full day
Day of Mindfulness
The full Plum Village format — sitting, walking, eating, sharing, and noble silence woven through the day.

A reading journey

Recommendations shaped by where you are in your practice. Not a curriculum to complete — a path to wander.

01
New to practice

Start here if you've just returned from retreat, or you're curious and want somewhere honest to begin.

The Miracle of Mindfulness
Thich Nhat Hanh
Bringing awareness into ordinary daily activities — washing dishes, walking, breathing.
Peace Is Every Step
Thich Nhat Hanh
Short reflections for meeting everyday life — the kitchen, the car — with more presence.
Wherever You Go, There You Are
Jon Kabat-Zinn
A secular, widely-read entry point into mindfulness meditation.
Interbeing
Thich Nhat Hanh
The fourteen guidelines of the Order of Interbeing — traditional ethics for contemporary life.
No Mud, No Lotus
Thich Nhat Hanh
On being with suffering without being overwhelmed — transformation, not avoidance.
Love Letter to the Earth
Thich Nhat Hanh
A call for a spiritual revolution grounded in love — treating the earth as relationship.
02
Deepening your practice

For you if you've been sitting regularly and feel ready to go beneath the techniques.

The Heart of the Buddha's Teaching
Thich Nhat Hanh
A clear grounding in the Four Noble Truths and the Eightfold Path. For readers ready to understand where the practice comes from.
True Love
Thich Nhat Hanh
On the four elements of real love — loving-kindness, compassion, joy, and freedom — and how they deepen through consistent practice.
03
Practice in the world

For you if sitting on a cushion no longer feels like enough — when the crisis outside calls for something.

Zen and the Art of Saving the Planet
Thich Nhat Hanh
Mindfulness applied directly to the climate crisis — clarity, compassion, and the courage to act without burning out.
Active Hope
Joanna Macy & Chris Johnstone
A four-stage practice for facing the crisis without collapsing into despair or denial. Best read together.
